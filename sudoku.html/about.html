About Zen Sudoku

Noticing what could be better. Deciding to build it.

I tend to look beyond how things work and wonder how they could work better. While exploring Sudoku games online, I found myself wishing for something with more thoughtful design and a little more variety. So, I decided to build my own version.

I enjoy logic, patterns and understanding how different pieces fit together. With Zen Sudoku, I wanted to combine that analytical side with an experience that feels calm, intuitive and thoughtfully designed. Because functionality is important, but so is the experience around it.

The project is still growing. I'm working towards puzzle generation, database storage and saved game progress, with plenty of ideas left to explore.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, this is my little space for experimenting, problem-solving and learning by creating. I'm rarely short of things I'd like to improve.

Sometimes, the most interesting part of solving a puzzle is figuring out how to build one.
