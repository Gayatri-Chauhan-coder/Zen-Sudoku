YOUR GUIDE TO THE PUZZLE

How to Play

New to Sudoku? Don't worry. Let's understand the rules and find your rhythm.

The Objective

Sudoku is a logic-based number puzzle. Your goal is to fill a 9 × 9 grid with numbers from 1 to 9 while following three simple rules.

Every puzzle starts with a few numbers already filled in. Use logic and observation to complete the remaining empty cells.

The Three Golden Rules

01

Rows

Each horizontal row must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.

02

Columns

Each vertical column must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.

03

3 × 3 Boxes

Every smaller 3 × 3 box must also contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.

How to Solve a Puzzle

1

Observe the Grid

Look at the numbers already provided and identify the empty cells.

2

Find Missing Numbers

Check the corresponding row, column and 3 × 3 box to find which numbers are missing.

3

Fill the Empty Cells

Select an empty cell and enter the number that logically belongs there.

4

Complete the Puzzle

Continue solving until every cell is filled correctly.

A Few Tips Before You Begin

  • Start with rows or columns that have more numbers already filled in.
  • Look for numbers that have only one possible position.
  • Avoid guessing. Let logic guide you.
  • Take your time and enjoy the process.
